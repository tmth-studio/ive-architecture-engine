# Verification boundary

The verifier is intentionally narrower than the architecture method.

It checks structure, traceability, provenance declarations, financial arithmetic and a published FMOS threshold. It cannot establish that a customer will buy, an intervention is causally sufficient, a cited source is true, or that an expert endorses the result.

The generator may propose architecture. It may not alter `rules/policy.json`, the verifier, score thresholds, or a report for its own artifact. Changes to those files require an independent review and a version bump.

## WS1 qualification status

This project is not evidence that WS1 is achieved. The WS1 qualification claim requires three consecutive, autonomous, held-out venture runs at the defined threshold with both structural gates clear, plus independent expert confirmation of one run. Until then, use the term **mechanically verified against v0.1** only.
